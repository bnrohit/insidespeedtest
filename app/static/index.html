Optional device troubleshooting

A completed speed test automatically records limited browser/network measurements for GMCS troubleshooting. For deeper local details—hostname, adapters, MAC addresses, DNS, route, ping, traceroute, ARP and listening ports—download and run the appropriate read-only script on your own device.

The browser cannot start a downloaded script automatically. Once IT approves and signs the script, launch it yourself or through an approved managed deployment. It saves a report to your Desktop; with a one-use code, it can securely submit that report to the matching test after you confirm. Do not disable Smart App Control or certificate checks.
